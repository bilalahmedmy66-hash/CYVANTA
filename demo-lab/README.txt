CYVANTA LOCAL DEMO LAB

Purpose:
A harmless local-only web app for testing CYVANTA's asset, authorization,
mission-planning, and audit workflows.

Address:
http://127.0.0.1:8765

Health check:
http://127.0.0.1:8765/health

Network safety:
The server binds to 127.0.0.1 only. It is not exposed to other computers
on your network or to the public internet.

It does not scan, exploit, collect credentials, or control other systems.

To start:
  python demo_app.py

To stop:
  Press Ctrl+C in the PowerShell window.
